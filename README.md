# News24 Coalition Calculator

Check which coalitions can reach a majority in each of South Africa's 213 metro and local councils, or build your own,
using the council seats from the 2026 local government elections.

- **Live page:** https://inertiacreep.github.io/coalition-calculator/
- **Data:** the Electoral Commission of South Africa (IEC): results declared on 8 November 2026, checked against the IEC's
  Seat Calculation Detail reports; registered voters and turnout from the IEC's Voter Turnout reports.
- **Rules page:** Local Government: Municipal Structures Act 117 of 1998, Municipal Systems Act 32 of 2000, Constitution
  s 160 and s 139, and the Municipal Structures Amendment Bill [B23—2026] (not law).

The page is a single self-contained HTML file. It sets no cookies and loads nothing from other sites. The only request it
makes is optional: when a reader shares a coalition with the "send a copy to News24" box ticked, it sends the council and the
parties chosen (nothing personal) to News24's tally.
